# Premium and high-end mattress / bed-set pricing at South African bricks-and-mortar chains and established brands (observed 6 Oct 2026)

Method note: All prices are ZAR incl. VAT as displayed on live retailer pages, scraped on **6 Oct 2026** ("observed Oct 2026"). Sleepmasters and Dial-a-Bed block direct fetches from this environment, so their pages were retrieved through a rendering scraper (Firecrawl). The scraper read the category listings with an LLM extraction step, and a spot-check found that this step sometimes **swaps sale and "was" prices** (Dial-a-Bed Dunlopillo listing vs. product page; see Q2 Gaps). Prices confirmed on individual product pages are marked **(PDP-confirmed)**. SA standard sizes used by retailers: Queen 152×188 cm, Queen XL 152×200 cm, King 183×188 cm, King XL 183×200 cm; Super King is sold only in 200 cm length at Dial-a-Bed. I did not capture its width. "Set" means mattress + base. "Sale / was" means current promotional price / strike-through "regular" price.

## Q1. What do the premium ranges of the main SA manufacturer brands cost (Sealy, Simmons, Rest Assured, Edblo, Restonic, Slumberland, Cloud Nine, Tempur, others) in queen, king and XL, mattress-only vs set?

### Takeaway
Two premium tiers exist. Sealy Crown Jewel, Serta, Restonic and Rest Assured's top lines sit at about **R13k–R21k for a queen/king set**. Simmons' and Sealy's import-style flagships (Simmons Winchester / Healthsmart / Smart Response / Beautyrest Black, Sealy Graphene Touch) run from **about R25k to R130k per set**. Tempur mattress-only prices run **R47k–R92k**. Most manufacturers (Cloud Nine, Sealy SA, Tempur SA) don't show prices on their own sites, so retailer pricing is the de facto market price.

### Inferences
- The Mattress Warehouse's page metadata gives the Crown Jewel Lindsay price range as "R 11,999.13 – R 25,825.22". R11,999.13 × 1.15 = R13,799, the displayed price, so the range is ex-VAT. - Sealy Graphene Touch Firm is R44,999 mattress-only (lowest listed size) and R54,999 as a Queen XL set. That suggests the Graphene mattress-only price is probably the queen price and the base adds about R10k. This is not confirmed.
- Simmons is the only brand with a clear ladder above R40k at a mass chain. Sealy's ladder jumps from Crown Jewel / Skyline (about R18k–R31k) to Graphene (R55k+), leaving a gap in firm sets at roughly R32k–R54k that only Simmons Healthsmart and Sherbourne fill (both medium/plush).

## Q2. What do the main SA bed retailers charge for premium lines, regular vs "sale"?

### Takeaway
**Dial-a-Bed** is the main mass-chain outlet for ultra-premium beds. It has 465 bed-set SKUs, a ceiling of R129,999 and a permanent sale of roughly 15–20% off "was" prices. **Sleepmasters** (Pepkor Lifestyle) tops out at about **R21k for a king set**, and its king listings mostly show a single price. **The Mattress Warehouse** and independents (The Bed Shop, Beds Online, The Gallery, Luxury Centre) sell Sealy Crown Jewel at **R10k–R30k**. Design-led furniture chains (Weylandts, Coricraft) sell premium bed frames, but I found no verified mattress pricing from them.

## Q3. Which premium lines are firm / orthopaedic / heavy-duty / latex / hybrid, and what premium do latex and extra-length command?

### Inferences
- The XL premium is a fairly flat **R1,000–R3,000**, not a percentage. On a premium set that works out to roughly 5–12%, and Simmons builds it into the price. A 210 cm length has **no off-the-shelf equivalent** at SA chains: every chain listing tops out at 200 cm, and Tempur at 200 cm (Euro sizing).
- 160 cm width is also non-standard for SA chains (152 or 183 cm). The nearest Euro equivalents are Tempur's 150 and 180 cm.
- At the chains, the only explicit weight rating I found was 150 kg per side (Dunlopillo). Ratings of 200 kg+ come only from factory and specialist sellers, mostly priced below R15k. A **250 kg-per-person rating** would therefore be above anything I found from an established brand or chain.
- Firm sells at no premium over plush. Plush and pillow-top versions often cost more (e.g. Graphene Firm vs Luxury: −R10k), so "firm" alone doesn't justify a higher price. Heavy-duty capacity, extra length and materials (UHD foam, latex, cotton) would have to carry it.

### Inferences
- In practice, **R20k is the line between chain premium and specialist premium**. Sleepmasters has almost nothing above R21k, while Dial-a-Bed has dozens of SKUs from R20k to R130k.
- Firm, large, extra-length sets from brands shoppers trust cluster at:
  - **R19k–R31k:** Sova, Skyline Extra Firm, Winchester Firm
  - **R55k–R70k:** Graphene Touch Firm, Smart Response Firm
- A custom 160×210 heavy-duty set priced at **about R25k–R35k** would sit alongside Simmons Winchester Firm (Queen R25,599 / King R30,799) and Sealy Skyline. A price of **R40k+** would put it against Healthsmart, Graphene and Tempur, where brand prestige and cooling or "smart" technology are the selling points rather than load rating. This is an inference for positioning, not a finding.
- Heavy-duty 200 kg-rated products from factories and specialists sell at **about R6k–R15k**. Big Jo's would need to justify a 2–3× premium over them with materials, the 250 kg rating, the 210 cm length, brand and service.
