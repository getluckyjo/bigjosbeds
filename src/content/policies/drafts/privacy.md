## What we collect

**When you buy a bed**

- your name, email address and mobile number
- your delivery address and any access notes you give us
- what you ordered and what you paid
- the payment notification PayFast sends us, which includes the payment reference, status and amounts, and the name and email address you gave PayFast

We never see or store your card or banking details. PayFast collects those under its own privacy policy.

**When you send us an enquiry**

- your name, email address and, if you give it, your phone number
- your area, the finish you’re interested in and your message

**When you message us on WhatsApp**

- your phone number, your WhatsApp profile name and your messages

These go straight to Johannes, our founder, on WhatsApp.

**When you visit the site**

Our hosting provider records technical details of each visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for, to run and protect the site.

We don’t use advertising or analytics cookies, and the site doesn’t load third-party trackers. During checkout, your browser stores a small marker in session storage so the payment page isn’t submitted twice. It’s deleted when you close the tab.

## Why we use it

- To take your payment, make and deliver your bed, and keep you updated about your order.
- To handle returns under the 100-day trial and warranty claims.
- To check that payments are genuine and to prevent fraud.
- To answer your enquiries.
- To keep the records we need for tax and accounting.

We only email you about your order or enquiry. We don’t send marketing emails.

## Who we share it with

We share only what each of these needs to do their job:

- **PayFast** (South Africa) processes your payment.
- **Supabase** stores order and enquiry records, on servers in Ireland.
- **Vercel** hosts this website. Our server code runs in Ireland.
- **Resend** (United States) sends our order and enquiry emails.
- **WhatsApp** (Meta) carries any messages you send us on WhatsApp, under its own privacy policy.
- **[TO CONFIRM: the business that makes and delivers the bed]** receives your name, phone number and delivery address so it can make and deliver your bed.
- Authorities, when the law requires it.

We don’t sell your personal information.

## Information sent outside South Africa

Some of these providers store or process information in Ireland, the United States and, for WhatsApp, other countries where Meta operates. We use providers that protect personal information under laws or binding agreements that give protection similar to POPIA.
