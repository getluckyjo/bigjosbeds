How big is the bed?
The mattress is specified at 160 × 210 cm, with an approximate depth of 25 cm. Use the exact dimensions when checking your room and linen.
What is the difference between the finishes?
Flax pairs a warm natural weave with clay stripes. Charcoal uses a dark grey weave with tonal stripes. Both designs keep the cream sleeping surface and piping.
How does it feel?
The product brief specifies a firm feel, with a layered foam support system and latex comfort layer.
