# Big Jo's Beds: brand background

## The business
Big Jo's Beds is a new Cape Town brand making premium beds for big and tall men. It will sell direct to customers, online first. The founder is Johannes Le Roux, a Cape Town entrepreneur who co-founded The Duchess alcohol-free drinks.

## The product
- **Mattress:** 160 × 210 cm, firm, rated for 250 kg per person.
- **Construction, from the bottom up:** 180 mm reinforced high-density support core, 50 mm ultra-high-density foam, 20 mm latex comfort layer, 100% cotton cover. About 25 cm thick.
- **Base:** a matching heavy-duty base.
- **Production:** made to order in Cape Town, in 3–5 working days.
- **Finish isn't final.** The manufacturer's sample has grey sides and a quilted white top. We'd prefer a clean, flat knitted top (like Sloom's).

## Who it's for
- Big and tall men, and their partners, who have outgrown standard beds. Their feet hang off a 188 cm bed, and their mattresses sag within a couple of years.
- Heavy men are the bigger group, and very tall men a smaller niche. "Big" covers both.
- They'll pay more for a bed that fits and lasts. They aren't hunting for a bargain.

## Why it's different
- **The size:** no one else in South Africa sells a 160 × 210 bed. Chains stop at 200 cm long, and 152 or 183 cm wide.
- **The weight rating:** 250 kg per person beats every local claim. Most premium brands rate to 150 kg, and the heavy-duty specialists to 200 kg.
- **The audience:** no South African bed brand speaks directly to big and tall men.

## Market and competitors
- **Sloom** (Cape Town, online) is the category leader. It has a clean, modern look, a queen mattress at R14,500, and a 150 kg rating.
- **Mr Mattress** (online and stores) sells the heavy-duty Ultra 200, rated 200 kg. It leads with specs and discounts, with sets at about R11k–R15k.
- **The chains** (Sleepmasters, Dial-a-Bed) sell Sealy, Simmons and Beautyrest. Premium sets run from about R13k to R70k, up to R130k at the very top, and are almost always "on sale".
- **International references:**
  - Big Fig (US) is built only for big bodies, with a founder story and pro-athlete ambassadors.
  - Saatva HD is a luxury heavy-duty mattress with natural latex.

## Positioning and tone
- **Price:** premium, above Sloom and Mr Mattress. The exact price is still being set.
- **Tone:** confident, warm and direct, with a bit of humour. Premium, but human rather than stuffy.
- **Size is a spec, never a problem.** Use lines like "built for big" and "finally, a bed that fits". Never use "bariatric", "obese" or "plus-size".
- **The name is friendly and folksy, so the design has to carry the premium.** That means restraint, good type, quality materials and real photography.
- **Avoid:** a discount-furniture look, a diner or tyre-shop "Big Joe's" feel, hospital or medical cues, and macho clichés.
- **Spelling:** it's "Jo's", not "Joe's".

## What the design needs to cover
- Logo or wordmark, plus a descriptor line (e.g. "Beds built for big men")
- Colour and type system
- Mattress side-panel label or embroidery, and the cover finish
- A product name for the mattress
- Website, product page and social media templates
- Delivery and unboxing experience, plus a spec and warranty card
