# 05 — Content governance

Source hierarchy: the user-approved board controls the visual direction; the supplied `design-brief-background.md` controls business/product background; this kit proposes web implementation details. The board is not evidence of a physical product's performance. No current market or competitor research is asserted by this kit.

| Fact / decision | Source | Publication treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Big Jo’s Beds; Cape Town; founder Johannes le Roux | Supplied brief | Brand background |
| Best sleep for big people. | Approved identity | Primary brand line; aspirational tagline |
| 160 × 210cm; firm; approx. 25cm | Supplied brief | Use as supplied specs; final product check before launch |
| 180mm support + 50mm foam + 20mm latex | Supplied brief | Technical spec; verify sample matches |
| 250kg per person | Supplied brief | Design rating; manufacturer substantiation pending |
| 100% cotton cover | Supplied brief | Material claim; verify final cover composition |
| 3–5 working days | Supplied brief | Production target only; confirm capacity; never delivery SLA |
| Flax and Charcoal appearance | Approved concept | Finish design direction; physical sample pending |
| Mattress + matching heavy-duty base | Supplied brief | Confirm sale configurations and exact inclusions |
| Price, warranty, returns, delivery, stock | Not supplied | Null / omitted until approved |
| Industry-first / highest rating claims | Unverified brief comparisons | Do not reuse without fresh substantiation |

Keep internal verification status in data and documentation, not as awkward customer-facing copy. If a launch claim cannot be substantiated, omit it or ask the owner. Do not turn unknowns into promises. This is a content policy, not legal advice or a template of commercial terms.
