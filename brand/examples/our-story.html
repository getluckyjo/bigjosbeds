About Big Jo’s

Some of us need a bigger bed.

For big and tall people, the usual bed-shopping questions come with a few extras. Will my feet hang off the end? What weight is the mattress designed for? Is the base strong enough?

Big Jo’s Beds starts there. We make a firm 160 × 210 cm mattress and matching heavy-duty base, made to order in Cape Town.

The size and support matter. So does how the bed looks in your room. The Flax and Charcoal finish designs use woven fabrics, simple stripes and a small sewn-on label.

Big Jo’s was founded by Johannes le Roux, a Cape Town entrepreneur and co-founder of The Duchess. The focus here is straightforward: beds for people who have outgrown the standard options.
