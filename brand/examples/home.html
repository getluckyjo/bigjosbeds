Your feet shouldn’t have to hang off the end.

If you’re big or tall, buying a bed means paying attention to things other people might never check: the extra length, the mattress rating, whether the base is up to the job.

Big Jo’s starts with those details. The mattress is 210 cm long and 160 cm wide, with a reinforced foam core, a latex comfort layer and a matching heavy-duty base.

Here’s what’s inside.

The mattress has three layers: a reinforced high-density support core, an ultra-high-density foam layer and a latex comfort layer. Together, they make up a firm mattress approximately 25 cm deep.

Started in Cape Town. Built for big.

Big Jo’s Beds was founded by Johannes le Roux, the Cape Town entrepreneur who co-founded The Duchess. This time, the brief is a bed for people who need more length and support than a standard bed offers.

About Big Jo’s

A few practical questions.

What size is the mattress?
160 cm wide by 210 cm long, with an approximate depth of 25 cm. Use those measurements when checking your room and linen.
Is it for tall people or heavier people?
Both. Big Jo’s is designed around the extra length and support that big and tall people look for in a bed.
How firm is it?
It’s a firm mattress, with a reinforced high-density support core, an ultra-high-density foam layer and a latex comfort layer.
